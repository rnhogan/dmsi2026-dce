About

This is a simple digital edition of Libro de arte coquinaria from the Library of Congress. The "Libro" is a 1460 manuscript full of recipes and it is believed to be from Northern Italy.

This edition was created during the "From Digitized Manuscript to Digital Edition" workshop at the Digital Medieval Studies Institute 2026 in Kalamazoo, MI during the International Congress of Medieval Studies. During the workshop, participants collectively transcribed the document and then used the transcript to each create a digital edition.

We used FromThePage for transcription and creating a plain text document, FairCopy for linking the text to the IIIF facsimilie from the Library of Congress and creating our XML document, and the EditionCrafter GitHub template for creating our websites.
